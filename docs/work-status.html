Each issue is listed once, in the table for its kind. Status, Priority, Difficulty, Estimate and Owner come from the issue's status:, priority:, difficulty:, estimate: and handler: labels; a closed issue's status comes from GitHub's close reason and its title is struck through. “Belongs to” is the issue's native parent. Search and the column filters work together and apply to every table; the tiles that show a count of Blocked, High priority or Partially implemented issues apply the same filter. A missing, conflicting or unknown value, or a parent link that loops back (“parent cycle”), is shown in red, never guessed, and an issue without a recognized product and kind label is listed as untriaged, whatever the filters. A table wider than the page scrolls inside its own section.
