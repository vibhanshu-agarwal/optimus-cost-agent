# 14. Context, authority and bounded-work guardrails

## 14.1 Inert model content

Ordinary conversation, prior model plans/replies, provider/tool text and generated summaries are untrusted as authority. A text claim cannot set permission, approval, effect state, test verdict or ledger completeness. The host supplies exact execution outcomes/effects and approval facts from its own records. Generated summary bodies never directly select workspace files or skills and never execute as a plan.

Exact retained ordinary plans/replies may contribute file/skill selection hints, resolved against current eligible workspace content under existing bounds. A path match proves neither an old diagnosis nor permission. Model/route switches do not trigger paid whole-chat audits, semantic tracking or a new watcher. Controlled edit paths retain current-target/version and approval checks; no universal protection against external writers is claimed.

## 14.2 Strategy refusal and recovery

Compaction/hybrid preserve their promised exact regions and protected facts or return unavailable. Do not silently degrade them into sliding or split a turn. Sliding deliberately omits older ordinary history, keeps canonical records, makes zero maintenance calls and gives the filed notice. A too-large newest sliding turn can leave no ordinary history in the view.

Missing/incompatible summarizers are unavailable, not transient failures to retry. Typed maintenance unavailable/unsupported statuses select the exact filed unavailable message. Real failed/rejected maintenance can retain chargeable receipts. The common refusal ending says that the thread stays open and no answer or plan was requested for that prompt; it does not claim no maintenance activity occurred.

For any unavailable prepared view, pure floor fallback is permitted only at <=524288 projected canonical bytes including the provisional prompt and only if the final packed request fits. Above that floor, refuse recoverably with no answer/planning dispatch. Repeated faults do not close the session or change its storage class. Protected authority is never dropped. A <=floor fallback is permitted only when its complete request fits; otherwise protected-state overflow refuses readably with OPEN disposition.

## 14.3 Bounded maintenance and stale work

Whole-turn packing obeys both token and UTF-8 byte bounds, with headers/separators and prior/future summary reservations. Preflight every chunk and the finite call allowance before dispatch. The final assembled-input guard remains. Fresh and reused summaries obey the same format/token/byte limits. A stale/cancelled/rejected/partial result publishes no checkpoint, while every actual provider attempt retains its accounting evidence.

The released offline test setting is at most 18 logical maintenance calls and one repack per turn, sharing the maintenance allowance rather than resetting it. These numbers require numeric-policy acceptance and supported route evidence. The fixture call bounds 17/9/5/9 are conditional on the pinned estimator/source/budgets and individually fitting turns; they are not a universal summarizability promise or a real latency guarantee.

The older cancellation diagnostic can still classify maintenance cancellation as failure internally; the ACP cancelled terminal remains correct. Keep that diagnostic residual visible in existing Plan 12 custody. Do not turn it into an undisclosed new code task or a blocker for the accepted correction batch.

## 14.4 Product dollars and evaluation dollars

After accepted D6/governed migration, product dispatch is constrained by trusted roles/routes, complete request capacity, output completeness, permissions, cancellation and finite work, not by a dollar threshold. Remove every equivalent dollar stop and remaining-dollar prompt, including non-ACP goal-loop monetary checks. Preserve actual costs and explicitly configured alerts. Unknown cost is explicit, known subtotal survives, and no automatic unknown-cost retry or session latch is introduced.

Keep current finite defaults of three planning rounds, 30 minutes and two repeated failures, plus single-call Chat. ADR-009's future detection/check-in/escalation design remains separate. A goal-loop iteration bound is not authorization to run that loop from ACP, and this migration does not select a new loop mechanism.

Evaluation/test spend caps remain independent authorization contracts. A live qualification or Task 13 harness stops on its approved call/dollar/time limits and preserves failure/unknown receipts. It may prohibit further evaluation calls when costs become unknown without introducing that prohibition into product session policy. No rerun-until-pass or paid candidate search is authorized implicitly.

## 14.5 Launch and evidence boundaries

A trusted effective registry object, not an untrusted caller's claimed hash, controls endpoint eligibility. Unverified endpoint/window/output/quantization/estimator/finish facts remain unavailable. Contributor payload disclosure follows the filed contract even for maintenance or recovery. CORE never obtains provider credentials. Each actual attempt revalidates the final packed input and one output reserve at the Gateway boundary.

An ephemeral real-client test composition is distinct from production attachment/bootstrap activation. The approved test workspace, scoped launch identity, credential ceremony and real dependency set must be recorded before live work. Do not bypass launch approval, repurpose unrelated services, start/stop other lanes or use fake clients to claim acpx/Zed evidence.

Coverage floors and full-dataset requirements are authoritative in Test Strategy §8A. Historical measurements do not prove persistent enforcement; retain failures, skips and UNRUN claims in closing evidence.



## 14.6 Maintenance request boundary

The 131072 estimated-token limit applies only to engine-assembled text. Host summary-prompt framing (989 bytes at the reviewed baseline), wire/framing and one effective output reserve must also fit the enabled Gateway’s complete-request guard. Finite count/time/failure controls belong to the host. Production enforcement and route activation remain separate from the reviewed test composition.
